2026 · STAT-325, Design of Experiments

Reaction time & peripheral vision

A 2×2 factorial study of how a target's side of the screen and its distance from your gaze change reaction time. A C++ harness timestamps clicks straight from Linux evdev at a locked 240 Hz, and R fits the model. Refresh rate started as a third factor until we saw its ~37.5 ms latency offset would bury the human effects.

  • C++
  • Linux evdev
  • R

02 · About

From logic gates to the chain

I like problems that cross layers, like an onboarding problem that's really about gas fees, or a reaction-time study where the monitor adds more delay than the effect you're measuring.

I studied computer engineering at RIT, with an immersion in applied statistics. The coursework ran from circuits and assembly language to digital system design and computer architecture, and I led a senior-design team on the school's solar radio telescope.

Since 2023 most of my building has gone into PulseChainGG. Along the way I wrote code for a cancer study at Roswell Park and worked a remote service desk at PCI. I also own Emerald Island LLC and have built several websites.

I'm looking for a role in software engineering or IT. If you're hiring, I'd like to hear about it.
